STEP 1: RESOLUTION EXTRACTION
Extract each director standing for election or re-election from the Step 1 JSON input, identifying:

- Full Resolution String (e.g., "Resolution 4a – Election of Ms Saw Teow Yam")

STEP 2: INTERNAL GOVERNANCE & ASA BENCHMARK AUDIT
For each candidate, cross-reference the Annual Report against ASA Guidelines across these 5 pillars:
1. Independence: Assess tenure (Flag if >12 years), former executive status (Flag if executive within past 3 years), or material commercial/family relationships.
2. Workload Capacity: Calculate exact ASA points:
   - Non-Executive Director (Listed) = 1 point
   - Board Chair (Listed) = 2 points
   - Paid NFP / Government Role = 1 point
   - Maximum allowable load = 6 points.
3. Shareholder Alignment: Calculate ownership percentage target: `(Shares Held × Current Share Price) ÷ Annual Base Director Fee`. Target: ~20% fee accumulation per year of service (100% by Year 5).
   - DATA FALLBACK PROTOCOL: If the exact closing share price or base director fee is not explicitly stated in the inputs, estimate based on available Remuneration Report disclosures and state your assumption clearly (e.g., "[Assumed $X base fee based on p. Y disclosures]").
4. Board Fit & Skills: Evaluate primary contributions against the Board Skills Matrix and board diversity goals.
5. Prior Performance: Audit disclosures for committee attendance (<90% threshold), governance failures, or prior ASA 'Against' recommendations.

STEP 3: EXTERNAL DUE DILIGENCE & LIVE WEB SEARCH (PAST 10 YEARS ONLY)
Execute real-time web searches covering strictly the past 10 years from today's date.
- Query Strategy: Combine `"[Director Full Name]"` with terms like `"[Company Name]"`, `"board"`, `"governance"`, `"lawsuit"`, `"regulatory"`, `"ASIC"`, `"APRA"`, `"controversy"`.
- Qualified Sources ONLY: Major financial press (e.g., AFR, WSJ, FT, Bloomberg, Reuters), regulator notices (ASIC, APRA, AUSTRAC), court judgments, and official announcements.
- Exclusions: Message boards (HotCopper, Reddit), social media (X, LinkedIn comments), unverified blogs, or anonymous rumor sites.
- Clean Profile Rule: If no material adverse findings exist, explicitly output:
  "No material adverse findings or external governance issues identified in qualified media/regulatory databases"

STEP 4: SOURCE INTEGRITY & BOARD SCRUTINY ASSESSMENT
For any external issues found via live web search:
- Citation: Include source name, date, and full Markdown link `[Source Name](URL)`.
- Fact vs. Speculation Test: Classify as substantiated fact (court ruling, regulatory penalty) vs. unverified media reporting.
- Board Scrutiny Test: Determine if the finding represents a material governance risk for board consideration.
